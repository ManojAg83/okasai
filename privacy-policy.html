1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how [Company Legal Name] ("OKAS", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the OKAS mobile app (the "App") and the related services at okas.ai.

The App lets you sign in, select a home or building (a "project"), and control its connected devices. These include lights, climate, covers, media, door locks, alarms and cameras. You can also manage the people who have access to the home.

By using the App, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to provide and secure the service. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

  • Sign you in and keep you signed in. This includes verifying one-time codes and Google or Apple sign-in, and showing the projects you have access to.
  • Run your home. We send your commands to your devices, show live device states, and run your scenes, schedules and favourites.
  • Manage access. Administrators use it to invite users, assign roles and room permissions, set access schedules, and view activity logs.
  • Send notifications. These include alerts and updates about your home, plus silent background messages that keep the App's data in sync.
  • Set up your home. We store room and panoramic photos so you can see and use rooms in the App.
  • Remember your preferences, such as language and theme.
  • Keep the service secure. We detect expired sessions and protect accounts against misuse.

Voice input: When you tap the microphone, your speech goes to your phone's built-in speech recognition service (usually provided by Google) and is converted to text. That service's own privacy policy applies. The App does not record or store audio, and does not send audio to OKAS servers. The in-app assistant runs on your device and does not send your text to any AI provider.

Legal bases (where required, e.g. GDPR): performing our contract with you or your organisation; our legitimate interest in securing and improving the service; and your consent for microphone access and notifications, which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings.

4. Local Network, Device Control and Intercom

When you are at home, the App talks directly to your OKAS home hub over your local Wi-Fi network. This traffic stays on your home network and does not pass through OKAS servers.

When you are away, commands and updates go through OKAS servers at api.okas.ai. This happens only if your administrator has given you remote access.

The intercom feature connects directly to the SIP server you set up. Your SIP password is kept in memory only while the App is running. Call logs are stored only on your device.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

The App keeps a local copy of some data so it opens quickly and works on your home network:

  • Sign-in session: access token, its expiry and your basic profile, kept in encrypted storage (AES-256).
  • Selected home and its layout: floors, rooms, devices, scenes, schedules and the users of that home.
  • Cached images: project, room and user photos.
  • Settings: preferences, favourite colours, the push-notification registration and the last sync point.

The App turns off Android cloud backup, so this data is not copied to your Google account backup. When you sign out, the App unregisters your device from notifications and clears the cached data and your session. Uninstalling the App deletes all locally stored data.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only in these cases:

  • Within your home or organisation. Administrators and project managers of a home can see its users, roles, permissions, access schedules and activity logs. Other users of the home can see shared things such as rooms, devices, scenes and device states.
  • Your installer or integrator. The organisation that set up your home may access its configuration to support it.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf, under contract (see below).
  • Legal reasons. We may share information to comply with the law, enforce our terms, or protect the rights, safety or property of users or others.
  • Business transfers. If OKAS is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred under this Policy.

8. Security

Traffic between the App and OKAS servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Sign-in tokens on your device are stored in encrypted storage, and access is limited by role, by room and by schedule. Communication with your home hub on the local network relies on the security of your Wi-Fi network, so please keep it protected with a strong password. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account by contacting us using the details provided in Contact Us.

Once the request is verified:

  • The account and associated personal information will be deleted.
  • Access to the App will be permanently removed.
  • Some limited data may be retained if required for legal or security reasons.

If your account was created by your home's administrator, they can also remove you from the home.

11. Children's Privacy

The Admin user is responsible for managing access permissions for minors.

If you believe a child's personal information has been provided to us without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will delete it.
